# Surface brief: storefront and brand site (all routes)

Mode: Persuade (Home, Shop, Product), with Operate-grade cart and checkout.
Audience, job, action: Philippine adults 30 to 60 on phones from Facebook; decide to trust the brand and send an order or question by message or call.
Proof and content: logo, honest placeholders, no invented claims or prices.
Constraints: CLAUDE.md v1.6, front end only, Workers static assets.
Unresolved: real photography, real product data, shipping and payment details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A premium, feminine, mobile-first shop led by the logo's own world: raspberry lacquer, pink, gold and sparkle. The curated range leads and a person is one tap away. It refuses the generic beauty tropes: cream ground, gold arcs, gradient washes, eyebrow labels, icon-on-card grids.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, plum ink, raspberry for action and the single drenched hero, petal panels, claret footer, gold only as hairline and sparkle on raspberry or claret. Bodoni Moda headings, Hanken Grotesk body, 20px soft imagery, pill controls, hairline rules. The four-point sparkle from the logo is the one motif.
STORY: The visitor sees a short range, understands orders are confirmed by a real person, and taps Message to order or adds to cart.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Raspberry announcement bar, white header with logo badge, then a full-width raspberry field: sparkle, large white Bodoni headline left, two actions (Shop the range, Call), and the 4:5 image slot right inside a thin gold frame.
FORM: canon, the category standard played straight, standing exit chosen, then refined toward the brand on request; seed:99b3d2b3ae5ad4ae
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
