# Surface brief: storefront and brand site (all routes)

Mode: Persuade (Home, Shop, Product), with Operate-grade cart and checkout.
Audience, job, action: Philippine adults 30 to 60 on phones from Facebook; decide to trust the brand and send an order or question by message or call.
Proof and content: logo, honest placeholders, no invented claims or prices.
Constraints: CLAUDE.md v1.9, front end only, Workers static assets.
Unresolved: real photography, real product data, shipping and payment details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A premium, feminine, mobile-first hybrid store led by the logo's own world: soft petal, claret, raspberry, gold and sparkle; it sells products and shows the business behind them. The curated range leads and a person is one tap away. It refuses the generic beauty tropes: cream ground, gold arcs, gradient washes, eyebrow labels, icon-on-card grids.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, plum ink, petal fields and panels, claret for the hero headline, announcement bar and footer, raspberry for action only, rosewater as a single plate behind the hero image, gold only as hairline and sparkle. Bodoni Moda headings, Hanken Grotesk body, 20 to 32px soft imagery, pill controls, hairline rules. The four-point sparkle from the logo is the one motif.
STORY: The visitor sees a short range, understands orders are confirmed by a real person, and taps Message to order or adds to cart.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Claret announcement bar, white header with logo badge and a collections bar on desktop, then a soft petal hero: sparkle, large claret Bodoni headline left, two actions (Shop the collections, Call), the 4:5 image slot right on a rosewater plate inside a gold hairline, and a small Get matched card overlapping it. The whole hero fits above the fold at 1440 by 900.
FORM: canon, the category standard played straight, standing exit chosen, then refined toward the brand on request; seed:99b3d2b3ae5ad4ae
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
