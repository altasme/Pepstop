# Surface brief: storefront and brand site (all routes)

Mode: Persuade (Home, Shop, Product), with Operate-grade cart and checkout.
Audience, job, action: Philippine adults 30 to 60 on phones from Facebook; decide to trust the brand and send an order or question by message or call.
Proof and content: logo, honest placeholders, no invented claims.
Constraints: CLAUDE.md v1.4, front end only, Workers static assets.
Unresolved: real photography, real product data, shipping and payment details.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A clean, premium, mobile-first shop where the curated range leads and a person is one tap away. It refuses the beauty-site tropes the old build used: cream ground, serif display, gold arcs, gradient washes, icon-on-card feature grids.
OWN-WORLD: White ground, one near-black plum ink, brand raspberry from the logo for action only, a pale petal tint for panels, hairline rules. One grotesk family, tight tracked display, sentence case. Flat surfaces, square-cornered product imagery, pill controls. Placeholders are flat hairline boxes with a diagonal cross, not decorative.
STORY: The visitor sees a small chosen range, understands that orders are confirmed by a real person, and taps Message to order or adds to cart.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim announcement bar, white header with logo badge, then oversized left-set headline, two actions (primary order action, secondary Shop), and a large image slot on the right at 4:5. On phones the headline and actions come first, image below.
FORM: canon, the category standard played straight, standing exit chosen; seed:99b3d2b3ae5ad4ae
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
